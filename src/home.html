GOOD QUESTIONS. STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

A little light
on the details.

New to solar? You're in the right place.

Does solar work on cloudy days?

Yes. Panels use daylight, not just direct sunshine. Output drops in cloudy weather, and winter generation is lower. A sensible design accounts for that variation.

Do I need a battery as well?

No. Solar can work on its own. A battery stores surplus generation for later, but its value depends on your usage, tariffs and installation cost.

Is my roof suitable for solar?

Orientation, shade, usable space and roof condition all matter. South-facing roofs are ideal; east and west can also work. A survey is needed before recommending a system.

How much will my system cost?

That depends on the design, equipment and installation requirements. The estimator shows an illustrative annual benefit, not a system price. Your proposal should itemise costs before you commit.

How reliable is the savings estimate?

It's a simple annual model to help you explore possibilities. It isn't a roof survey or a forecast. A tailored proposal needs location, shading, usage and tariff data, with costs included separately.
